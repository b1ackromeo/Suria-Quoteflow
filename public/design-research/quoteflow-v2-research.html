Suria QuoteFlow UI/UX research board

Move the product from admin template to commercial operations workspace.

QuoteFlow should feel like a working system for finance, sales, procurement, and management. The interface needs document queues, guided workflows, live document preview, company identity controls, approvals, and payment visibility without decorative dashboard noise.

Research signals applied

  • Procurement platforms prioritize request-to-payment visibility, approvals, receiving, vendor controls, budgets, and real-time status.
  • ERP quotation tools use list and form views, quick create actions, customer auto-fill, quotation templates, order lines, and PDF/send actions.
  • Invoice dashboards focus on receivables, overdue/current split, bills, payments, and fast drill-down from summary to exact document.
  • Modern Dribbble/Figma SaaS examples use split workspaces, clear tables, status chips, restrained palettes, dense readable rows, and fewer oversized cards.
Recommended direction A

Document Workbench

Best fit for quotation, PO, and invoice pages. The user selects one record from a serious document queue, then reviews the live professional document preview on the same screen.

Use workflows as navigation

QuoteFlow should not feel like unrelated CRUD pages. It should expose outgoing and incoming process stages clearly.

Keep document context visible

Users need the selected quote, PO, or invoice visible while they filter, approve, attach, convert, or generate PDFs.

Make company identity real

Suria QuoteFlow is the product. The active company is operational context and document identity.

Reduce decorative UI

Use dense lists, tables, status, and action panels. Avoid oversized hero/dashboard decoration that slows actual work.
